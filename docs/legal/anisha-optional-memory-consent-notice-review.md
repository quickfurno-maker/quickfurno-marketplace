## Proposed vendor-facing notice

**Optional memory for Anisha**

QuickFurno offers optional memory so Anisha can remember your requested **conversation language** (English, Hindi or Hinglish) and **reply style** (short or detailed) in future vendor support chats. You can still use ordinary vendor support without this option.

If you opt in, QuickFurno stores a small derived preference linked to your vendor account, with a maximum retention period of **30 days per preference record**. The feature does **not** intentionally retain your complete conversations as long-term memories or save customer phone numbers, payment details, exact credits, lead assignments or package balances as memory. Those business details, if needed, are fetched from current QuickFurno records subject to your permissions.

When Anisha answers a related question, the relevant language or reply-style preference may be included in the context processed by QuickFurno's AI service provider. Standard operational and security logs and the record of your consent are separate from optional preference memory; those may have different retention periods under the QuickFurno Privacy Policy.

**Your choice:** Select the unchecked consent box, then tap *Grant optional memory permission*. This permission is separate from accepting WhatsApp messages, general account terms or marketing. If you do not grant permission, Anisha may still respond, but will not use this optional retained preference memory.

**Withdraw at any time:** Open Vendor Dashboard → Support → Anisha memory preferences → *Withdraw consent and request erasure*. Future optional memory reads are blocked immediately. Stored preferences are then deleted through a verified process; the dashboard indicates when deletion is pending. Deletion completion is not represented as instantaneous.

To request correction or obtain help, use QuickFurno vendor support and see the [QuickFurno Privacy Policy](/privacy).
